How ReplaySafe handles information in this foundation build.

Data we store

  • Account data: email, password hash, organization name, API tokens.
  • Usage: incident create counts per organization per calendar month.
  • Incident packs: redacted request metadata, headers, bodies, and logs you submit.

Storage

Local deployments persist JSON under DATA_DIR (incident packs) and accounts.json beside that store. No paid telemetry vendors are required.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. The demo Bearer token demo is for local evaluation only.

Your choices

Contact the operator of your deployment for deletion or export. Replace this stub before processing customer PII in production.
